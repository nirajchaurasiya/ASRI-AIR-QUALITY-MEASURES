## Research Project

### Research Question

> Is there a significant difference in reported air-quality values between observations derived from **Monitor Only** data and observations derived from **Monitor and Model** data?

### Motivation

Air quality measurements are widely used by researchers, policymakers, and environmental agencies to assess environmental conditions and public health risks.

Different methodologies can be used to estimate air quality. Understanding whether these methods produce significantly different results is important because such measurements influence environmental assessments, policy decisions, and scientific research.

## Key Learning Outcomes

One of the biggest lessons from ASRI was learning that:

> Finding a pattern is different from proving a relationship.

A visualization may suggest a trend, but statistical analysis is necessary to determine whether that trend is meaningful.

Another important takeaway was that:

> Doing research and communicating research are two different skills.

A strong research project requires both rigorous analysis and the ability to clearly explain findings to others.
